# Game news

The latest posts from the developers of your Steam games, for
[EdgeStation](https://github.com/chainlist/edgestation): updates, patch notes and events.

- **Follow a game** opens a search of the Steam store, without DLCs, demos and soundtracks. Tap
  a result to follow it.
- Each game shows its latest post, newest first. A dot marks a post you have not read yet.
- **Tap a game** to read its posts, with images and lists, and step through its 5 latest.
  **Unfollow** is at the top of the reader.

Only the developers' own announcements show, not the press articles that Steam mixes in. The
news loads when its page first shows, then at most every 30 minutes while the page is on screen.
Steam needs no account or key for this. Each placement keeps its own games.
